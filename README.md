# AI and Employee Behavior: An Organizational Behavior Case Study at Techcombank

## Overview

This is an individual academic project completed as part of the Organizational Behavior course.

The project explores how artificial intelligence (AI) adoption may influence employee perceptions, work attitudes, motivation, and behavior in the context of Techcombank. Using a case scenario involving AI-assisted credit processing, I apply organizational behavior theories to examine employee responses to technological change and propose practical recommendations for managing the human side of digital transformation.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze how AI adoption may affect employee behavior and work motivation.
- Apply organizational behavior theories to explain individual, group, and organizational responses to change.
- Identify potential causes of employee anxiety, passive resistance, and reduced engagement.
- Propose practical recommendations to support employee adaptation and organizational effectiveness.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Individual Behavior and Motivation

Apply perception and job attitude concepts, Vroom's Expectancy Theory, and Self-Determination Theory to examine how AI may affect employees' professional identity, motivation, and willingness to adapt.

### 2. Group Behavior and Communication

Explore how communication gaps, informal rumors, and group dynamics may influence employee anxiety, collaboration, and acceptance of AI.

### 3. Organizational Culture and Change Management

Examine the relationship between organizational culture, role clarity, and change management, including Kurt Lewin's change model.

### 4. Recommendations

Propose practical measures involving employee reskilling, internal communication, change management, and updates to job descriptions and competency requirements.

## Key Takeaways

- Technology adoption requires attention to employee perceptions and psychological needs, not only operational efficiency.
- Employees may respond differently to the same technological change depending on their experience, confidence, and perceived career opportunities.
- Clear communication and role expectations can help reduce uncertainty and support adaptation.
- Training is more effective when combined with practical application, mentoring, and visible career development opportunities.
- Successful digital transformation requires alignment between technology, people, and organizational management practices.
